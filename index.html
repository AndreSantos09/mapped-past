Mapped Past Pipeline

A private, single-user publishing tool for the YouTube channel Mapped Past: original documentary explainers about history, geography and science.

The tool uploads and schedules my own videos through the YouTube Data API and reads my own channel analytics through the YouTube Analytics API. It is not available to the public and has no other users.
